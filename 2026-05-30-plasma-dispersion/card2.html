Section 2

R-Wave and L-Wave: Split Paths

n²R = 1 − ωp² / [ω(ω − ωc)]    n²L = 1 − ωp² / [ω(ω + ωc)]

A magnetic field splits plasma waves into two modes. The sign difference in the denominator — ω − ωc versus ω + ωc — produces radically different cutoffs and propagation windows. Two waves, two physics.
