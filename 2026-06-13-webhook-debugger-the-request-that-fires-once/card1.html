Card 1 / Why Webhooks Fail

Three patterns, every incident

1. Signature mismatch on the first prod event

The secret in staging is whsec_test_123. The production secret is a 32-byte random value copied into three places -- one still has the test secret.

2. Body parser strips the raw payload

Express's bodyParser.json() runs before Stripe middleware. req.body is parsed, not raw. The HMAC was over raw bytes. Verification fails on every request.

3. Replay protection rejects the test payload

Stripe allows 5 minutes. By morning, the timestamp has aged out and the signature is invalid by design.
