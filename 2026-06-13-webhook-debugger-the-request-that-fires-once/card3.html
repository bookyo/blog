Card 3 / Conditional Replay

From passive logger to active proxy

1. Point upstream at the debugger's capture URL instead of your endpoint.

2. Set bodyMustContain to "type":"invoice.paid". Leave autoReplay off.

3. Trigger the upstream test event (Stripe CLI stripe trigger invoice.paid).

4. Inspect the captured request: headers, HMAC verdict, JSON shape.

5. Flip autoReplay on. Forward every matching request to your real endpoint.

6. Watch your endpoint's logs. If it 500s, flip off, fix, replay manually from the buffer.

You debug on your clock -- not on the upstream's retry policy.
