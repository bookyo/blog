Wetting vs. Repelling

Water on clean glass: θ ≈ 30° — it spreads and wets. Water on paraffin wax: θ ≈ 105° — it beads and resists. Mercury on glass: θ ≈ 140° — complete non-wetting. Same liquid, different surfaces, entirely different behavior.
