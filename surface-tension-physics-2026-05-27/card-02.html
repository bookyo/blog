The Silent Pump in Every Plant

Water climbs 14 cm in a 0.5 mm glass tube — and 70 cm in a 0.1 mm tube — without any pump. Xylem vessels in trees (20–200 μm diameter) use this same physics to lift water from roots to the highest leaves.
