CARD 03 · HORIZON

5,000,000 Years

Approximate Lyapunov time of the inner solar system.

We can integrate Mercury's orbit forward for a few tens of millions of years reliably. Past that, the sign of the Lyapunov exponent wins. Weather has a Lyapunov time of days. A healthy heartbeat has a negative exponent — fibrillating heartbeat, positive. The horizon of predictability is itself a dynamical number.
