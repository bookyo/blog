At r = 4, the logistic map has λ = ln 2.

Two starting points separated by 10⁻¹⁵ diverge to 1 in about 50 iterations. Every doubling takes exactly one step. That is the speed of unpredictability in a fully chaotic regime — a half-bit per iteration.
