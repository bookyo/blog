The Mathematics

Three Equations. No Randomness. Genuine Unpredictability.

dx/dt = σ(y - x)
dy/dt = x(ρ - z) - y
dz/dt = xy - βz

σ=10, ρ=28, β=2.67

Three parameters. Three variables. Completely deterministic. Yet it never repeats. Ever.

The Implication

The End of Laplacian Determinism

Before Lorenz, scientists believed the future was theoretically predictable — just a computational problem. Lorenz showed that for chaotic systems, no amount of computational power is enough. The universe contains pockets of genuine unpredictability baked into mathematics itself.

The butterfly in Brazil doesn't cause tornadoes. But the mathematics describing its wings does.
