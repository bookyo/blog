Diamond Sparkle: TIR at 24.4°

Diamond's high refractive index (n ≈ 2.42) gives a critical angle of only 24.4°. Most facets exceed this — light bounces inside until it exits through the top crown facets only.

n_diamond ≈ 2.42 | θc ≈ 24.4°

Well-cut diamond: light enters, undergoes TIR, exits only through top facets — producing characteristic sparkle
