Optical Fiber: Light Trapped by TIR

Fiber core (n ≈ 1.48) has higher refractive index than cladding (n ≈ 1.46). Light injected at the correct angle bounces down the fiber — less than 0.2 dB/km loss at 1,550 nm.

n_core ≈ 1.48 | n_cladding ≈ 1.46

Silica fiber loss: 0.2 dB/km at 1,550 nm — transoceanic without repeaters
