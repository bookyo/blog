THE FLOOR

A regex is the floor, not the answer

What 70% recall against real application logs actually looks like

A 70% recall rate is not 'good enough for compliance.' It is 'good enough that you will pass your first audit and fail your second.'
  • International formats the scanner doesn't know: Korean RRN, Brazilian CPF, Indian Aadhaar
  • Encoded PII: Base64, URL-encoded, JSON-stringified — none of them match the email regex
  • PII split across fields: first name + last name + phone — no single field matches alone
