CORE PRINCIPLE

The Fourier Foundation

A spectrogram is not a recording. It is a decomposition — a mathematical unwrapping of sound into its most fundamental parts.

  • • Time runs left to right; frequency runs top to bottom
  • • Color intensity = volume at that frequency and moment
  • • The Fourier transform makes the invisible visible

"The result looks almost like a topographical map, with bright ridges and valleys that trace patterns invisible to the naked waveform.
