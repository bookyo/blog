One Chord, A Thousand Lines

A piano chord sounds like a single entity. A spectrogram shows a precise constellation of horizontal lines at mathematically related frequencies.

  • • Fundamental note at the bottom — the perceived pitch
  • • Harmonic overtones ascend in diminishing brightness
  • • These overtone ratios define the instrument's timbre

"The relative strength of these overtones is what gives a piano its characteristic timbre — why it sounds different from a guitar playing the same note.
