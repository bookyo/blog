Bias Matters

Forward vs Reverse Bias

Forward bias narrows the depletion region — current flows exponentially. Reverse bias widens it — almost no current passes until breakdown at ~-100V.

Forward Bias
V > 0.6V → I flows exponentially
Depletion region narrows
Reverse Bias
V < 0 → I ≈ 0 (saturation)
Depletion region widens
