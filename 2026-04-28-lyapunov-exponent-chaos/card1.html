The Formula
Key Concept

Two Identical Systems.
One Number Decides Their Fate.

λ tells you how fast nearly-identical starting conditions drift apart — and whether prediction is even possible.

λ > 0: trajectories diverge exponentially. Chaos.
λ = 0: periodic motion. λ < 0: stable, predictable.

  • λ quantifies the exponential separation rate of nearby trajectories
  • λ < 0 means convergence — the system settles toward a fixed state
  • λ = 0 means periodicity — trajectories hold their separation
  • λ > 0 means chaos — deterministic but unpredictable in the long term
