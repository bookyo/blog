The Butterfly
Doesn't Cause
the Tornado

A positive Lyapunov exponent means the growth of uncertainty is exponential — not that tiny causes create huge effects arbitrarily.

For weather systems, λ ≈ 0.9 per day.
A perturbation doubles in size roughly every 18 hours.

  • Exponential divergence, not unbounded growth — that's the real meaning of chaos
  • Weather is unpredictable beyond 10-14 days not because models are bad, but because uncertainty compounds exponentially
  • The butterfly shifts initial conditions; exponential divergence does the rest
  • This is why long-range weather prediction is physically impossible, not just practically difficult
