λ Is Being Used to Predict Seizures, Market Crashes, and Grid Failures

The Lyapunov exponent isn't just theoretical — it's a real diagnostic tool applied across biology, finance, and engineering.

EEG readings show characteristic Lyapunov exponent shifts 15-30 minutes before seizures.

  • Epilepsy: λ analysis of brain activity provides seizure early warning
  • Power grids: cascade failures can be detected minutes before they become inevitable
  • Finance: fat tails in market returns emerge from slightly positive λ — not Gaussian randomness
  • Neural networks: learning is most effective at λ ≈ 0, the edge of chaos
