Glass Within Glass

The fiber core (n₁ ≈ 1.47) and cladding (n₂ ≈ 1.46) differ by only ~0.01. That tiny difference — controlled to nanometer precision — creates the boundary where TIR occurs. Light enters within the acceptance angle, then bounces millions of times without escaping.

NA = √(n₁² − n₂²) ≈ 0.168 — the numerical aperture that defines the acceptance cone
