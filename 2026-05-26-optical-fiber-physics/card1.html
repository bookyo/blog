The Core Law

n₁ · sin(θ₁) = n₂ · sin(θ₂)

Snell's Law describes every bend of light — from a spoon in water to a laser pulse in fiber. Light slows down in denser materials, and when it hits an interface at a shallow angle, it refracts. But only up to a point.

When n₁ > n₂ and the angle exceeds the critical value — light reflects instead.
