Adding Circles
Builds Squares

f(t) = (4/π) × [sin(t) + (1/3)sin(3t) + (1/5)sin(5t) + ...]
Odd harmonics only: 1, 3, 5, 7...
Amplitude decays as 1/n — higher harmonics fade
Infinite sum converges to a perfect square wave
Each circle spins at an integer multiple of the fundamental
