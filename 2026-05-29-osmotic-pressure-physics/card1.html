The Van't Hoff Equation

π = iMRT

π is osmotic pressure in atmospheres. M is molar concentration. R is the ideal gas constant. T is absolute temperature. i is the van't Hoff factor (1 for glucose, 2 for NaCl).

At 0.1 mol/L and 298 K: π ≈ 2.4 atm — the pressure that naturally pushes water across every cell membrane.
