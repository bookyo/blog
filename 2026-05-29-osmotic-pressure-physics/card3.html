Reverse Osmosis

When Pressure Overpowers Entropy

Apply more pressure than the osmotic pressure, and the direction reverses. Solvent gets forced out of solution. Seawater becomes drinking water.

Seawater at 0.6 M NaCl generates ~30 atm osmotic pressure. Desalination plants run at 50–70 atm to consistently overcome it. The same equation, inverted.
