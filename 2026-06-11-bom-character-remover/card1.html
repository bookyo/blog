The hex view that ends the search

0000: EF BB BF 7B 22 6E 61 6D

↑ UTF-8 BOM ↑ actual data starts here

A leading EF BB BF means the file has a UTF-8 BOM. Three bytes. Invisible to every text editor you'll open. Fatal to pandas.read_csv, JSON.parse, and most strict parsers.
