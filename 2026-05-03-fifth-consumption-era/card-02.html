THE DEMOGRAPHIC FACT

52% of Households Will Be Single-Person by 2040

The population structure driving fifth-era consumption is not a prediction — it is already embedded in the birthrate and household data of every wealthy nation.

  • Single households: 23% (1990) → 52% (2040)
  • Population over 65: 12% (1990) → 39% (2040)
  • Birthrate: 1.54 (1990) → 1.15 (2040)
