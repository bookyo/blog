Luhn Algorithm

Luhn is for catching typos, not stopping attackers

The IMEI check digit has been a Luhn mod-10 sum since 2003. It catches every single-digit error and most transpositions — which is exactly the job it was designed for in 1960.

1
Start from the rightmost digit and double every second digit going left (skip the check digit itself).
2
If a doubled value exceeds 9, subtract 9 from it (or sum its digits).
3
Add all the doubled and undoubled digits together.
4
If the total is a multiple of 10, the IMEI is structurally valid.

Treating Luhn as a security feature is a category error. The real fraud defense lives in the carrier Equipment Identity Register and the GSMA TAC database, not in a 60-year-old checksum designed for tired clerks.
