Hidden Twist

The 16-digit version nobody warned you about

Since 2017 the GSMA has been rolling out IMEISV — a 16-digit format where the last two digits are a software version, not a Luhn check digit. Most validators get it wrong.

15-digit IMEI
490154203237518
Classic format. Last digit is Luhn-protected. Structural validation works.
16-digit IMEISV
35-209900-176148-2
Newer format. Last two digits are the radio software version in hex — no Luhn check applies.
↓

A naive validator counts 16 digits, runs Luhn, gets a wrong answer, and flags a valid device. The free IMEI Validator on Elysia Tools handles both formats — it strips separators, recognizes the IMEISV pattern, and reports the TAC and serial portions without demanding a Luhn match that does not exist.
