Structure

The 15 digits are not random

An IMEI is a structured identifier. The first 8 digits form the Type Allocation Code, then 2 digits for the unit serial, then the Luhn check digit.

The first two digits (RBID) identify the reporting body — 49 is Japan, 35 is UK, 86 is China. The next six name the model. Digits 9 and 10 are the unit serial. The last digit is Luhn-protected.
