Umbra & Penumbra
Core Concept

Two Shadows,
One Moment

"Stand inside the umbra and you see totality. Stand in the penumbra and you see a partial eclipse."

Umbra: cone of total darkness, 100–160 km wide on Earth
Penumbra: larger diffuse shadow surrounding the umbra
Lunar eclipses cast Earth's larger shadow — lasts over an hour
Solar umbra races across Earth at up to 2,000 km/h
