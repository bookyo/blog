Why Eclipses Are Rare
Key Insight

5° Makes
All the Difference

"Most months the new Moon passes above or below the Sun. Only at eclipse seasons does the alignment snap into place."

Moon's orbit tilted ~5° to Earth's solar orbital plane
Two eclipse seasons per year — windows of ~34 days
Total solar eclipses visible only in 100–160 km wide track
Lunar eclipses visible from entire night hemisphere
