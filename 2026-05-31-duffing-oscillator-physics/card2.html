Fractal Geometry

Poincaré Sections

Snapshots at Every Driving Cycle

Take a snapshot once per driving cycle. In periodic motion, every snapshot lands on the same point. In chaos, they land on different points — but always along a curve. Zoom in and that curve is made of thinner curves, folded again and again. This self-similarity at every scale is the hallmark of fractal geometry — connecting the Duffing oscillator to coastlines, price charts, and a mathematics of roughness.
