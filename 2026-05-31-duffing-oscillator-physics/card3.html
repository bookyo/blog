Jump Resonance

When Small Changes Produce Sudden Jumps

In a linear spring, doubling the drive amplitude roughly doubles the response. In the Duffing system, the relationship breaks down. As you slowly increase the drive amplitude, the response amplitude follows — then jumps discontinuously to a different value. The system can occupy two different states at the same drive amplitude, depending on how you arrived there. This jump resonance is not an imperfection. It is the system revealing its nonlinearity.
