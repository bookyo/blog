The Four Strokes
Part 02

Two Isothermal
Two Adiabatic

"A rectangle on the P-V diagram — each side represents a distinct thermodynamic process."

Isothermal expansion: heat absorbed at T₁
Adiabatic expansion: gas cools T₁ → T₂
Isothermal compression: heat rejected at T₂
Adiabatic compression: warms back to T₁
