FOUR ALGORITHMS

How an ID
checks itself

Weighted-sum

China, Japan, Germany — multiply each digit by a permuted weight, take the sum mod N.

Luhn-modulo

India Aadhaar — alternating double-and-reduce, total mod 10 must be zero.

Forbidden letters

UK NINO — the valid alphabet is smaller than the possible one.

Region & structure rules

US SSN — 000, 666, 900–999 area numbers are reserved, never issued.
