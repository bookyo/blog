Clouds Stay White
Because Size Matters

"Rayleigh scattering (1/λ⁴) applies to particles smaller than the wavelength — air molecules. Larger particles use Mie scattering, where all colors scatter equally."

Rayleigh: molecules smaller than wavelength — blue dominates
Mie scattering: particles larger than wavelength — white dominates
Fog lights use yellow (580 nm) to penetrate fog with minimal scatter
