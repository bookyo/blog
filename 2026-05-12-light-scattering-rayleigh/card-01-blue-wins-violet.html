Blue Beats Violet
by 9.4×

"Violet scatters more than blue, yet the sky is blue — not purple. Three reasons explain why our eyes see what they do."

Sunlight is weaker at violet wavelengths — the solar spectrum peaks in blue-green
Human eyes have 3× more blue-sensitive cones than violet-sensitive ones
The upper atmosphere absorbs violet light before it reaches your eyes
