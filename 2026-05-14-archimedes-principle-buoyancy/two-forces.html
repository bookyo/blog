Gravity vs Buoyancy: The Silent Tug-of-War

"Gravity pulls down. Buoyancy pushes up. The moment they match, the object stops accelerating."

Gravity = mg (object's weight)
Buoyancy = ρfluid × V × g (weight of displaced water)
Equilibrium: Fb = mg — object hovers
Shape controls displaced volume → controls buoyancy
