Same Equation, Different Geometry

Both wave types satisfy the same wave equation and share the same frequency, amplitude, and propagation speed. The only difference is the direction of particle oscillation — perpendicular for transverse, parallel for longitudinal. Adjust the frequency slider and both waves respond in lockstep, proving the shared underlying mathematics.

Transverse Wave

Oscillation ⊥ propagation

Light, ocean waves, S-waves

Longitudinal Wave

Oscillation ∥ propagation

Sound, P-waves, compressions
