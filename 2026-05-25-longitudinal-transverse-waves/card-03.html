Two Wave Types, Two Seismic Waves

Earthquakes radiate both P-waves (primary, longitudinal) and S-waves (secondary, transverse). P-waves arrive first and travel through the liquid outer core. S-waves cannot pass through liquid — their shadow told geologists the core was molten before anyone drilled toward it.

P-Waves

Primary · Longitudinal · Compression

Travel through solids AND liquids

S-Waves

Secondary · Transverse · Shear
