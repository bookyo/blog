ZT: The Key Number

ZT = S²σT / κ

The thermoelectric figure of merit ZT combines Seebeck coefficient (S), electrical conductivity (σ), temperature (T), and thermal conductivity (κ). Higher ZT means more efficient conversion. ZT = 1 → ~10% of Carnot. ZT = 3 → ~20%. Voyager's RTG uses silicon-germanium at ZT ≈ 1.

η = η_Carnot × (√(1+ZT)−1)/(√(1+ZT)+Tc/Th)
