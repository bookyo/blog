The Seebeck Effect

Heat Gradient → Voltage

When two different conductors form a junction and a temperature difference exists across it, electrons drift from the hot side to the cold side, generating a voltage proportional to ΔT. This is the quiet engine at the heart of Voyager's RTG.

V = S × ΔT

S = Seebeck coefficient (µV/K)
