The Proof That Chaos Is Not Rare

Sprott's Exhaustive Search: 19 Simple Systems

Julian Sprott ran a computer search in 1994 for the simplest possible chaotic flows. Case A: dx/dt = y, dy/dt = −x + yz, dz/dt = a − y². One quadratic nonlinearity. A single parameter. This is all it takes for chaos to emerge.

His search found 19 distinct simple chaotic systems (A through S) — proving chaos is not a pathological curiosity but a universal feature of simple dynamical systems.
