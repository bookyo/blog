The Simplest Chaotic System

The Rössler Attractor: Three Lines of Calculus

Otto Rössler designed the simplest chaotic autonomous system in 1976. Three differential equations. One quadratic nonlinearity (zx). The rest is pure geometry — a ribbon that winds and folds infinitely without ever crossing itself.

dx/dt = −y − z
dy/dt = x + ay
dz/dt = b + z(x − c)

Parameters: a=0.2, b=0.2, c=5.7
