One Equation, One Plaza

Piet Hein built Sergels Torg in Stockholm from the superellipse in the 1960s.

  • Stockholm's central plaza since 1968
  • Shape rejected both circle and square
  • Hein: "The eye prefers continuous curvature"

"The superellipse, Hein argued, was the most aesthetically pleasing intermediate between a circle and a rectangle."
