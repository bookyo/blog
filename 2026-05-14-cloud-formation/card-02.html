Three Clouds,
Three Climates

"Same rain icon. Completely different physics — and completely different outcomes for your afternoon plans."

Cumulus — Fair weather, flat base, cauliflower tops. Base at 600–2000m
Stratus — Gray blanket, stable air, light drizzle. Base at 0–600m
Cumulonimbus — Towering 12,000m+ storm clouds with anvil tops
Each type reveals the lapse rate winner: stability vs. instability
